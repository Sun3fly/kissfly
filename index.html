Research & Industry Experience

OBSBOT2026.06 – 2026.09

Multimodal LLM Algorithm Intern

  • Built a robotic-arm cinematography LLM on top of a Qwen3.5 multimodal backbone, translating user shooting requests into arm trajectories that drive the camera to perform cinematographic moves.
  • Encoded camera-motion primitives with Finite Scalar Quantization (FSQ) and merged the learned codebook with the Qwen vocabulary into a unified token space, adding two learnable special tokens to delimit motion-token spans.
  • Designed a two-stage LoRA training pipeline: stage one predicts a caption of the camera motion from the arm state and subsequent actions, stage two predicts action chunks step by step from the arm state, the user instruction and the current camera image.
  • Constructed an 18K-trajectory training set and a 200-trajectory benchmark; the model executes six basic camera moves (push/pull, left/right, up/down) from arbitrary initial arm states with accurate adherence to commanded distance and speed.
Hikvision2026.01 – 2026.05

Large Language Model Algorithm Intern

  • Implemented and tuned knowledge-distillation algorithms for a self-developed multimodal LLM, building the training framework on the verl library to support multiple distillation recipes.
  • Distilled a 4B model into a 1B student on ChartQA-style data and systematically studied off-policy / on-policy / GKD distillation, temperature, learning rate, epochs and supervision forms (forward KL, reverse KL, JSD, veto) to identify the most profitable configuration.
  • Improved average overall accuracy by about 7% across seven ChartQA-style benchmarks with on-policy distillation.
  • Built a 4B safety expert model: collected and merged recent open safety benchmarks into the evaluation framework, generated teacher responses with Qwen3-235B-A22B, and fine-tuned the 4B model with SFT for later multi-teacher distillation.
